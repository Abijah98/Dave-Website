About David Goodman

David Goodman writes novels and short stories about families, coastlines and the objects people leave behind. He grew up in a harbour town, taught high school English for twelve years, and now writes full time from a converted boathouse.

He answers every reader email himself, and runs a free monthly workshop for new writers.
